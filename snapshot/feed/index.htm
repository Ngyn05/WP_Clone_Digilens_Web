Future-Proof Your XR Strategy: Launch of ARGO Next Program by DigiLens Enables the Shift from Any XR Device to the ARGO Platform

Partnership with Altoura provides customers the ability to seamlessly transition from any XR device, like the discontinued Microsoft HoloLens, to the ARGO platform, while retaining their current Microsoft infrastructure

Sunnyvale, CA – October 7, 2025: DigiLens Inc., a leader in next-generation waveguide display technology and extended reality (XR) smartglasses, has launched the ARGO Next program, a critical step in future-proofing enterprise XR strategies as organizations everywhere face Microsoft HoloLens 2 end-of-life. In partnership with Altoura, the newest member of the DigiSaaS marketplace and a leading AI-powered frontline platform, ARGO Next enables seamless migration from any XR device, such as HoloLens, to enterprise-ready smartglasses, DigiLens ARGO, with full compatibility across existing Microsoft cloud infrastructure. Businesses can upskill their workforce with easy access to immersive SOPs, product manuals, and next-generation collaboration, bringing in a new era of productivity, training, and knowledge management.

“Microsoft pioneered an impressive frontline productivity transformation with HoloLens and their software solutions,” said Jamie Fleming, Altoura CEO. “At Altoura, we’re excited to see DigiLens step forward with ARGO—bringing customers next-generation hardware that pairs seamlessly with Altoura Frontline to make the transition effortless. Together, we’re ensuring that enterprises can continue to scale knowledge, empower their frontline workers, and accelerate productivity with future-ready technology.”

ARGO Next: Providing HoloLens Customers a Path Forward

With Microsoft pivoting away from mixed reality hardware and HoloLens 2 officially unsupported after December 31, 2027, organizations must urgently replace legacy hardware without sacrificing years of investment in XR training, operations, and integration. Through ARGO Next, DigiLens and Altoura deliver a direct, future-ready migration path, ensuring business continuity with a familiar, enhanced experience and expanded functionality. Altoura Frontline is the replacement solution for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Guides (Frontline Procedures) and Remote Assist (Frontline Remote Expert).

Key Benefits

  • Optimized for ARGO: Native hardware support for seamless migration
  • Cross-device compatibility: Continue using current XR devices during migration with no disruption to daily workflows
  • AI-powered authoring: Create and update SOPs in hours, not days, accelerating rollout
  • Advanced collaboration: Leverage spatial 3D annotations, secure sharing, and desktop collaboration for remote and frontline teams
  • Automated migration: Free, API-level integration for continuity

“With the AR industry at a turning point, enterprise customers demand solutions that combine proven hardware with scalable, intuitive software, and our partnership with Altoura delivers exactly that,” said Brian Hamilton, VP of Sales and Marketing at DigiLens. “Our partnership with Altoura delivers exactly that – empowering organizations to migrate from HoloLens and deploy next-generation AR workflows. At DigiLens, we’re focused on giving customers the confidence to protect their investments, modernize operations, and stay ahead in a fast-changing market. Together, we’re setting a new standard for enterprise XR migration and productivity.”

DigiLens Helps Transform Enterprises Through 3D Visualization and Real-Time Collaboration Capabilities on ARGO with Hololight

DigiLens will deliver multimillion polygon models to mobile extended reality (XR) hardware, enabling workers to seamlessly visualize and collaborate in real-time to perform a wide array of tasks, improving efficiency and decision-making. This includes allowing customers to stream Omniverse content and 3D models to ARGO with Hololight streaming, enhancing interactive experiences and operational workflows.

Sunnyvale, CA – September 23, 2024: DigiLens Inc., a leading nanotechnology innovator in waveguide display technologies and XR smartglasses, announced today it is bringing complex, high-fidelity, multimillion polygon models to DigiLens ARGO smartglasses in collaboration with Hololight. Transforming how industries engage with 3D content and enabling real-time collaboration on mobile XR hardware will allow workers to shift from workstations to enterprise-grade XR smartglasses, as high-quality 3D assets can now be securely visualized anytime and anywhere, enhancing design and engineering processes. Workers can visualize and collaborate in real-time to perform tasks like reviewing designs, planning factory layouts, assessing risk, quality assurance, and more to improve decision-making.

Most mobile XR devices have only been able to render up to one million polygon models natively and run into issues like overheating and greatly reduced battery life. ARGO’s all-in-one system paired with the NVIDIA Omniverse platform and Hololight Space means ARGO can support more robust 3D models — up to 30M polygons tested so far — without quickly overheating and draining power. Omniverse, a platform for developing OpenUSD applications for industrial digitalization and generative physical AI, renders the complex models. Hololight Space’s integrated proprietary streaming technology, Hololight Stream, enables highly secure, high-fidelity visualization without the need for data optimization on ARGO.

“Our work with DigiLens allows us to push the boundaries of 3D visualization and real-time collaboration. With Hololight Space, users can now securely experience unprecedented high-fidelity 3D content on ARGO, revolutionizing the way industries design, plan, and innovate,” said Florian Haspinger, CEO of Hololight. “ARGO’s all-in-one system and Hololight Space’s seamless integration with NVIDIA Omniverse will help set a new standard for 3D content management and visualization.”

ARGO is the first purpose-built, standalone AR/XR device designed for enterprise and industrial-lite workers. NVIDIA Omniverse enables developers to easily integrate Universal Scene Description (OpenUSD) into existing software tools and allows users to enhance their 3D applications and deploy them anywhere from NVIDIA Omniverse Cloud. Hololight Space is a leading industrial augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) software for seamless 3D data visualization.

By offloading the processing to Omniverse and leveraging Hololight Space to visualize and stream high-fidelity 3D content to ARGO effortlessly, users will have a centralized repository for all their assets and can take advantage of the security benefits of not having the content stored locally on the device. Workers can utilize ARGO’s spatial capabilities to walk around, interact with, and view different layers and details of complex digital twin models such as jet engines, machinery, hospital scans, and more.

“Hololight’s streaming solution integrated with NVIDIA Omniverse unlocks new possibilities for digital twin models in hyper-scaled environments for many use cases,” said Nima Shams, DigiLens VP and GM of Product Engineering. “Up to now, AR products were rooted for consumer solutions. DigiLens has developed a purpose-built solution platform, tailored for industrial markets, that’s poised to become the primary choice for workers.”

DigiLens Partners with Avegant to Deliver Compact and Manufacturable Full-Color Transparent Displays for Next-Generation AI Smartglasses

This strategic collaboration aligns cutting-edge light engine technology with the global leader in scalable waveguide manufacturing, performance, and price

Silicon Valley, CA — September 4, 2024: DigiLens Inc., a leading nanotechnology innovator in waveguide display technologies and XR smartglasses, has teamed up with Avegant, a leading expert in light engine technology, to provide transparent, efficient, and affordable displays for the next-generation of AI-enabled smartglasses. By combining DigiLens’ holographic waveguide technology with Avegant’s light engine expertise, customers can design lightweight and stylish augmented reality (AR) smartglasses, perfect for the consumer market.

Begin your evaluation journey today by purchasing an Insight Kit directly from DigiLens. The Insight Kit includes the DigiLens Crystal30 waveguides and the Avegant AG-30L2 AR light engine, allowing customers to swiftly create and view content through the module. The combination of Avegant’s light engine and DigiLens’ waveguides delivers a daylight-bright image of over 3,000nits.

“Avegant is excited to join the DigiLens ecosystem as they are known to be leaders in performance and cost for waveguides, making them an ideal partner for AI consumer smartglasses,” said Ed Tang, CEO of Avegant. “DigiLens can manufacture waveguides at scale with high yield that can enable consumer price points. The combination of DigiLens and Avegant delivers a 1,000nits display at only 75mW of illumination power”.

DigiLens’ efficient waveguides power consumer, enterprise, and industrial products like smartglasses, helmets, and other headworn devices, and can accommodate various light engines based on customer preferences. The Crystal30 is backed by DigiLens’ global licensee network, enabling large-scale volume manufacturing to meet consumer market demands.

Avegant’s AG-30L2 AR light engine is extremely compact, efficient, and high-resolution, featuring liquid crystal on silicon (LCoS) technology with a volume as low as 1.4cc. This light engine supports small and sleek form factors and utilizes proprietary illumination technology to significantly reduce engine volume and weight while preserving efficiency and modulation transfer function (MTF).

“Light engines drive every AR product,” said Chris Pickett, DigiLens CEO. “Building upon years of light engine research, design, and manufacturing, Avegant offers cutting-edge technologies that bring compact, efficient, and manufacturable AR light engines to the marketplace.”

Insight Kits can be purchased from DigiLens by submitting an inquiry online or emailing sales@digilens.com.

Avegant’s AR Light Engines will enable customers to provide a compelling AR experience in a consumer wearable AR device. For more information, visit avegant.com.
