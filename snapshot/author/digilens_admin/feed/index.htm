Bảo vệ chiến lược XR tương lai: Chương trình ARGO Next của DigiLens cho phép chuyển từ mọi thiết bị XR sang nền tảng ARGO

Partnership with Altoura provides customers the ability to seamlessly transition from any XR device, like the discontinued Microsoft HoloLens, to the ARGO platform, while retaining their current Microsoft infrastructure

Sunnyvale, CA – 7 tháng 10, 2025: DigiLens Inc., a leader in next-generation waveguide display technology and extended reality (XR) smartglasses, has launched the ARGO Next program, a critical step in future-proofing enterprise XR strategies as organizations everywhere face Microsoft HoloLens 2 end-of-life. In partnership with Altoura, the newest member of the DigiSaaS marketplace and a leading AI-powered frontline platform, ARGO Next enables seamless migration from any XR device, such as HoloLens, to enterprise-ready smartglasses, DigiLens ARGO, with full compatibility across existing Microsoft cloud infrastructure. Businesses can upskill their workforce with easy access to immersive SOPs, product manuals, and next-generation collaboration, bringing in a new era of productivity, training, and knowledge management.

“Microsoft pioneered an impressive frontline productivity transformation with HoloLens and their software solutions,” said Jamie Fleming, Altoura CEO. “At Altoura, we’re excited to see DigiLens step forward with ARGO—bringing customers next-generation hardware that pairs seamlessly with Altoura Frontline to make the transition effortless. Together, we’re ensuring that enterprises can continue to scale knowledge, empower their frontline workers, and accelerate productivity with future-ready technology.”

ARGO Next: Providing HoloLens Customers a Path Forward

With Microsoft pivoting away from mixed reality hardware and HoloLens 2 officially unsupported after 31 tháng 12, 2027, organizations must urgently replace legacy hardware without sacrificing years of investment in XR training, operations, and integration. Through ARGO Next, DigiLens and Altoura deliver a direct, future-ready migration path, ensuring business continuity with a familiar, enhanced experience and expanded functionality. Altoura Frontline is the replacement solution for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Guides (Frontline Procedures) and Remote Assist (Frontline Remote Expert).

Key Benefits

  • Optimized for ARGO: Native hardware support for seamless migration
  • Cross-device compatibility: Continue using current XR devices during migration with no disruption to daily workflows
  • AI-powered authoring: Create and update SOPs in hours, not days, accelerating rollout
  • Advanced collaboration: Leverage spatial 3D annotations, secure sharing, and desktop collaboration for remote and frontline teams
  • Automated migration: Free, API-level integration for continuity

“With the AR industry at a turning point, enterprise customers demand solutions that combine proven hardware with scalable, intuitive software, and our partnership with Altoura delivers exactly that,” said Brian Hamilton, VP of Sales and Marketing at DigiLens. “Our partnership with Altoura delivers exactly that – empowering organizations to migrate from HoloLens and deploy next-generation AR workflows. At DigiLens, we’re focused on giving customers the confidence to protect their investments, modernize operations, and stay ahead in a fast-changing market. Together, we’re setting a new standard for enterprise XR migration and productivity.”

RemoteSpark Officially Certified on ARGO Platform, Redefining the Future of the Digitally Connected Workforce

Now available through DigiSaaS, RemoteSpark của Kognitiv Spark trao quyền cho các đội ngũ ở mọi nơi, giúp tổ chức vận hành linh hoạt, bền vững và tăng khả năng thích ứng số.

Sunnyvale, CA – 4 tháng 9, 2025: DigiLens Inc., a leading nanotechnology innovator in waveguide display technologies and XR (extended reality) smartglasses, announces that RemoteSpark by Kognitiv Spark, a member of DigiSaaS, is officially ARGO-certified, marking a key milestone in its continued deployment on DigiLens ARGO. Built with end-users in mind, RemoteSpark is the leading connected worker support platform for the industrial and defense sectors, streamlining workflows, fostering seamless collaboration, and boosting productivity while reducing downtime and improving operational efficiency. Optimized for ARGO, the solution is designed for effortless implementation, regardless of the organization’s level of experience in deploying heads-up display technologies.  

ARGO + RemoteSpark: Delivering a Future-Ready Solution

ARGO and RemoteSpark’s purpose-built capabilities can operate in demanding environments at a high level of precision and durability. Through the ARGO platform, RemoteSpark can deliver a reliable, robust, low-bandwidth solution, equipping modern workforces with the best tools to drive confidence, efficiency, and resilience on the frontline. Features include:

  • Seamless integration with Microsoft Teams and OneDrive: Provides flexible connectivity options and allows organizations to utilize tools they already have
  • Real-time remote support and collaboration: ARGO’s Crystal waveguide display and sensors provide a high-resolution experience to all, ensuring the job gets executed right the first time
  • Simple hands-free information access: Keep important information directly in your field of view with RemoteSpark’s built-in PDF viewer, supported by ARGO’s spatial sensors
  • High-resolution asset capturing and sharing: ARGO’s 48MP center camera can capture critical details, while remote experts can add live photo annotations, enhancing remote diagnostics and feedback

Industrial-grade AR is Not Just a Fad – It’s Here to Stay. 

“The DigiLens and Kognitiv Spark partnership is more than just compatibility–it’s a shared vision. When organizations can get early results like reduced downtime and higher quality of work from their workforce, AR transitions from a novelty to a necessity,” said Brian Hamilton, DigiLens VP of Sales and Marketing. “Clear first use ROI is the tipping point for AR adoption, and Kognitiv Spark is leading the way.”

The solution is already deployed with several customers, including the UK Ministry of Defense, with a focus on enhancing situational awareness, real-time decision-making, and operational effectiveness.

“Truly transformational technology can create an impact while almost fading in the background,” said Yan Simard, CEO of Kognitiv Spark. “RemoteSpark on the ARGO creates that seamless experience field workers are looking for, while generating near immediate, tangible benefits.”

Through DigiSaaS, DigiLens is creating an ecosystem of solutions designed to not only meet organizational needs where they are now, but more importantly, where they’re going. With Kognitiv Spark, ARGO can deliver multi-level, sustainable impact to organizations by alleviating core issues, such as improved knowledge transfer, reduced equipment downtime, and lower travel costs. By combining ARGO, the first rugged, purpose-built, all-in-one AR and AI smartglasses, with solutions like RemoteSpark, workforces can achieve more and operate with confidence, solving issues quickly, safely, and accurately. 

To experience RemoteSpark on ARGO, schedule a live demo during DSEI 2025 in London, September 9-12, by contacting  Adam Clay (adam@kognitivspark.com), or contact DigiLens at engage@digilens.com.

Manifest Joins DigiSaaS, Enhancing DigiLens ARGO Ecosystem for Frontline Workers

Taqtile’s Manifest is now available on ARGO through DigiSaaS, accelerating digital transformation for frontline and deskless workers across industries

Sunnyvale, CA – 10 tháng 6, 2025: DigiLens Inc., a leading nanotechnology innovator in waveguide display technologies and XR (extended reality) smartglasses, is excited to announce that Taqtile’s Manifest is now a part of the DigiLens DigiSaaS marketplace. This partnership brings powerful AR workflow and training capabilities to organizations deploying ARGO, enabling the digitalization of frontline and deskless workers.

DigiSaaS is the official marketplace for the ARGO platform and future DigiLens products, designed to accelerate the adoption of XR technology by connecting solution partners with DigiLens’ global customer base and channel network. Through DigiSaaS, ARGO customers gain access to a vibrant ecosystem of certified, industry-specific, and ARGO-optimized software solutions, enabling them to maximize their return on investment in AR technology and drive operational transformation within their industry. Certified solutions span multiple industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, field service, and education, enabling use cases such as AI, data visualization, workflow guidance, real-time consultations, and more.

“Pairing Manifest with ARGO redefines what’s possible for frontline teams,” said Dirck Schou, Taqtile Co-Founder and CEO. “Taqtile is excited to be a DigiSaaS member, as many of our customers in the defense, manufacturing, aerospace, and other sectors are already showing strong interest in ARGO as the next-gen AR platform.”

Taqtile Manifest in DigiSaaS delivers an AI-powered, AR-enabled work instruction platform to more frontline workers than ever. Manifest simplifies expert knowledge sharing with step-by-step guidance, annotated photos and videos, AR objects, and built-in remote assistance – all in one powerful work instruction platform.

Optimized for ARGO, Manifest empowers operators, technicians, and maintainers to complete complex tasks quickly, accurately, and confidently, even in demanding environments. From equipment maintenance in defense and aerospace to procedures in manufacturing and utilities, this collaboration brings a scalable, hands-free solution for frontline teams, right where the work happens.

“Welcoming Taqtile to the DigiSaaS marketplace is a major milestone for DigiLens and the ARGO ecosystem,” said Brian Hamilton, VP of Sales and Marketing at DigiLens. “Manifest is a proven platform for delivering immersive, hands-free digital workflows to the frontline, whether on the factory floor or in the field. This partnership brings even more value to our customers by dramatically improving task execution, training, and knowledge transfer through spatially aware, AR-enabled work instructions. We’re excited to see the impact this collaboration will have across industrial and defense sectors.”

Key Benefits for DigiSaaS Partners

  • Global reach: Access to DigiLens’ worldwide salesforce and channel network
  • Technical enablement: Receive expert guidance to optimize and certify solutions for ARGO
  • No cost to join: Lowering barriers for emerging solution providers to join and scale

By creating this marketplace and partner program, DigiSaaS will enable partners to reach new customer segments and industries that otherwise may be difficult to access, and help XR customers discover innovative XR software solutions, accelerating technology-driven innovation across industries.

Solution providers are invited to join DigiSaaS to unleash their full potential on the ARGO platform and become a part of the future of spatial computing. To learn more, go to digilens.com/digisaas or contact engage@digilens.com.

To experience Manifest on ARGO, schedule a live demo at Augmented World Expo in Long Beach, California, June 10-12, by contacting sales@taqtile.com.

DigiLens ARGO Training AI Agents with Real-World Data in Partnership with Ramblr.ai®

Các tác nhân AI cấp doanh nghiệp của Ramblr tận dụng hệ cảm biến tiên tiến của ARGO và màn hình ống dẫn sóng Crystal hàng đầu để hỗ trợ tác vụ theo thời gian thực và tự động hóa tài liệu cho nhân sự doanh nghiệp, công nghiệp

Sunnyvale, CA – 15 tháng 5, 2025: DigiLens Inc., a leading nanotechnology innovator in waveguide display technologies and XR smartglasses, and Ramblr, an innovative AI solutions provider, announced a strategic partnership to enable AI for DigiLens ARGO by providing end-to-end workflow guidance utilizing ARGO’s multimodal sensor suite, visual odometry (VO) and 6DoF capabilities. This collaboration will transform work captured on ARGO into an automated workflow and provide AI-assisted evaluations in real-time, revolutionizing the enterprise and industrial workforce.

The Future of the AI-Powered Workforce
ARGO, known for its industry-leading XR smartglasses capabilities and crystal-clear waveguide displays, paired with Ramblr’s video AI agents, will provide the ultimate toolkit for enterprise and industrial workers by providing real-time, context-aware intelligence. Ramblr’s AI agents allow workers to access visual representations of AI-driven analysis and insights captured from ARGO, improving decision-making and operational efficiency, and drastically reducing the workload of supervisors in evaluating worksite jobs.

From fully automated AI-powered workflow guidance to real-time supervision, ARGO and Ramblr will empower workers to operate smarter, faster, and confidently.

“This partnership is a turning point—AI Agents on smartglasses will transform how we work,” said Roman Hasenbeck, Ramblr CEO. “Powered by ARGO’s sensor stack, Ramblr delivers hands-free, context-aware AI to the workforce, bringing state-of-the-art technology to teams long excluded from the benefits of advanced AI.”

A Different Kind of AI
Ramblr introduces a new kind of AI experience—built to perceive, interpret, and act in the physical world in real time. Powered by an in-house trained VLM (vision-language model), Ramblr’s end-to-end platform allows workers to interact naturally with AI, ask it questions about the process or task, and receive immediate, contextually aware responses through ARGO. ARGO’s advanced multi-camera system enables Ramblr’s AI Agents to capture rich situational context, track key objects and process steps—even as they move in and out of view—ensuring consistent and reliable video understanding. Powered by the Ramblr Data Engine, these AI Agents can be trained, monitored, and continuously adapted to new workflows through state-of-the-art automation and expert-in-the-loop capabilities.

“Partnering with Ramblr is a game-changer for DigiLens and our DigiSaaS solutions marketplace. AI agents on smartglasses empower seamless integration between the digital and physical worlds and pave the way for a more connected and intelligent future,” said Brian Hamilton, DigiLens VP of Sales and Marketing. “This partnership will unlock new possibilities for our customers, such as significantly decreasing onboarding time by utilizing digital workflows and enhancing their ability to interact with the work environment more intuitively. We look forward to seeing the transformative impact this partnership will have for our customers.”

DigiSaaS: The Official Marketplace for DigiLens ARGO Solution Partners
Ramblr joins the growing list of solutions in the DigiSaaS-certified marketplace. DigiSaaS connects ISVs (independent software vendors) to DigiLens’ global channel network and customers. By bringing together unique solutions optimized for the ARGO platform, DigiLens is enabling the next generation computing platform and helping DigiSaaS members unlock their solution’s potential.

For additional information on DigiSaaS, visit digilens.com/digisaas
